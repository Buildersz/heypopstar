Popstar Karaoke App Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what the Popstar karaoke app for Android and iOS ("Popstar", "the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Popstar is made by Buildersz. If you have questions, email popstar.karaoke.support@gmail.com.

1. What Popstar does

Popstar helps you find karaoke videos on YouTube, build a song queue, host or join a karaoke party, cast to a TV, and record your own performance.

2. Information we collect

Google account. You sign in with Google. Popstar receives your name and email address and read-only permission to search YouTube on your behalf. The sign-in token is kept on your device. Popstar does not run its own account system.

Party Mode. When you host or join a party, the following is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database so everyone in the party can see it: the party code, the song queue (song titles, video IDs, thumbnails), the display name of the person who added each song, and leaderboard scores (Android only). Party data is deleted when the host ends the party.

On your device. Favorites, your queue, recent searches, recently played songs, settings, and any recordings you make are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

Microphone. The app uses the microphone only if you choose to record your performance or, on Android, to use scoring. Recordings are saved on your device. They are only shared if you tap Share and choose where to send them. Audio is not uploaded to us.

Camera. The camera is used only to scan a party QR code. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.

Local network. Popstar looks for Chromecast / Google Cast devices on your Wi-Fi network so you can cast to a TV.

Advertising and diagnostics. The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, ad interactions, and crash and performance data. On iOS, Popstar requests non-personalized ads and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites. On Android, ad personalization follows your Google settings.

3. How we use information

  • To sign you in and search YouTube for karaoke videos.
  • To run Party Mode and show the queue and leaderboard to party members.
  • To play, cast, and record songs.
  • To show ads that help keep the app free, and to keep the app stable.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In and YouTube API Services. Popstar uses YouTube API Services. By using Popstar you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your use of Google services is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Popstar's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Google Firebase for Party Mode data.
  • Google AdMob for advertising.
  • Google Cast for casting to a TV.

5. Retention and your choices

  • Delete your data: open Settings in the app and tap Delete my data. This removes your favorites, queue, recent searches and settings from your device, disconnects Popstar from your Google account, and signs you out.
  • Party data is removed when the host ends the party.
  • Recordings can be deleted by uninstalling the app.
  • You can also email popstar.karaoke.support@gmail.com to ask about your data. Our account and data deletion page has more detail.

6. Children

Popstar is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use reputable services (Google Firebase, Google Sign-In) and keep as little data as possible. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
